# Sales performance Analysis Dashboard
## Project Objective 
To analyze sales and profitability performance across products, customer segments, shipping modes, and regions using Power BI, and identify key business areas that require further attention.

 # Questions
-	What are the overall sales, profit, orders, and profit margin? 
-	How do sales and profit vary across product categories and customer segments? 
-	Which products contribute the most to sales and profit? 
-	How do discounts relate to profit margin? 
-  How do sales and profit vary by shipping mode? 
-	How does sales performance change over time? 
-	Which regions and states perform better or lower in sales and profitability? 
-	Which regions have higher or lower profit margins?

# Process
-	Imported and prepared the sales dataset for analysis in Power BI. 
-	Created calculated measures using DAX for sales, profit, orders, average sales, and profit margin. 
-	Analyzed sales and profitability across categories, products, customer segments, shipping modes, and regions. 
-	Created interactive visualizations including bar charts, donut charts, line charts, scatter plots, treemaps, tables, and maps. 
-	Added slicers, filters, and navigation buttons to make the dashboard interactive. 
-	Developed three dashboard pages: Sales Analysis, Product Analysis, and Regional Analysis. 
-	Interpreted the results to identify products, regions, and business areas requiring further attention.

 # Project Insights
-	The dashboard recorded 9.43M in total sales and 1.11M in total profit, resulting in an overall 11.73% profit margin. 
-	Technology generated the highest sales among product categories, while Clothing showed the highest profit margin at around 27.41%. 
-	Consumer was the largest customer segment, contributing approximately 42.79% of sales. 
-	Printer was the top-selling product with 615.98K in sales, while Laptop generated the highest product profit at 53.43K. 
-	East recorded the highest regional sales, while South recorded the lowest sales among the four regions. 
-	East also recorded the highest regional profit at 315.63K. 
-	Regional profit margins were relatively close, with the highest regional margin reaching 12.06%. 
-	The dashboard also highlights the relationship between discount and profit margin, allowing areas with lower profitability to be investigated further. 

#  Final Conclusion
The analysis highlights product performance, regional performance, and profitability as key areas requiring attention. The business can further investigate lower-performing products and regions, while reviewing discount and profit-margin patterns to identify opportunities for improving overall profitability.
